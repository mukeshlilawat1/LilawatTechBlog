# Code of Conduct

All participants in the **LilawatTechBlog** project are expected to adhere to the following code of conduct.

---

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we pledge to:

* Be respectful and considerate
* Value differing opinions and experiences
* Gracefully accept constructive criticism
* Focus on what is best for the community
* Show empathy towards other community members

---

## Expected Behavior

* Treat everyone with respect
* Use welcoming and inclusive language
* Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
* Accept constructive criticism gracefully
* Focus on improving the community
* Show empathy towards other participants

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

* Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
* Offensive comments related to gender, sexual orientation, race, religion, or any personal characteristic
* Threats or abusive behavior
* Public or private harassment
* Other conduct which could be considered inappropriate

---

## Reporting Guidelines

If you encounter any unacceptable behavior, please report it by contacting the maintainers immediately. Reports will be handled confidentially.

---

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take appropriate actions, including warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal from the project, depending on the severity of the conduct violation.

---

Thank you for helping us maintain a safe and welcoming environment in **LilawatTechBlog**!
